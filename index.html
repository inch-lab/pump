Starting or just started a GLP-1 for the first time?

Help us study ways to support healthy eating among people taking GLP-1 medications. Participation is free and takes place in Lubbock, Texas.

See if you qualify

The short screener takes about 3 minutes.

You may qualify if:

  • You are 18–60 years old.
  • You recently started a GLP-1 medication for the first time OR you plan to start a GLP-1 medication soon for the first time.
  • You can attend 5 in-person study visits in Lubbock, TX.

What is included?

  • Free lab testing
  • Free body composition assessments
  • Free nutrition guidance
  • Free GLP-1 support and education
  • Up to $100 in payment for completing the entire study

Please note: The study does not provide or pay for your GLP-1 medication.

What is the commitment?

  • 5 in-person visits in Lubbock, TX over the course of 3 months.
  • 3 visits take about 1 hour each.
  • 2 visits are shorter.

Want to join?

  1. Complete the short screener. Answer a few questions to see if you may qualify.
  2. The study team contacts you. If you may be eligible, our team will contact you and explain the study.
  3. You decide whether to participate. Taking part is completely your choice.

Important to know

  • Taking part is your choice.
  • You can stop participating at any time.
  • The study team will explain everything before you start.
  • Your information will be kept private.
  • The study does not provide or cover the cost of your GLP-1 medication.
